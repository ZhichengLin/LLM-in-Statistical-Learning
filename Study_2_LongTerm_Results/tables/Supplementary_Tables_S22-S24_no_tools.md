# Supplementary Tables S22–S24 (no-tools eligibility; primary 33, sensitivity 39)

Generated by `code/build_supplementary_tables.py` from aggregate outputs of the frozen 44-submission snapshot. All long-term analyses are exploratory and were not preregistered. Two reporting decisions were made after long-term results were known: participants with both follow-ups form the primary sample, and self-reported use of external tools or materials (AI tools, websites, books or notes) while answering the long-term items is a mandatory eligibility exclusion (5 of 44 submissions; a self-report, not proof of unaided answering). Excluded participants’ records are omitted from every long-term analysis, including the full-cohort models (cohort of 170). The original initial-session (n = 175) and one-month (n = 84) results are unchanged historical results. Accuracy is the equal-weighted mean of the three task proportions (percentage points, pp).

### E. Missing-outcome sensitivity for the 170-person cohort

If each of the 131 missing 14-month outcomes could lie anywhere from 0 to 1, the cohort mean baseline-to-14-month change is only partially identified, within [−32.8, 44.2] pp (identification bounds, not confidence intervals). A zero cohort change would require nonreturners to average 42.6% at 14 months, versus their observed baseline mean of 46.8%. Neither MAR nor this scenario is verifiable. The cohort excludes the five tool users entirely.
